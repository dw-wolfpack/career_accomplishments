# TL;DR

I build the systems that train, serve, and evaluate models. I have also run pretrained PANNs for audio classification and embeddings, fine-tuned BERT, and deployed Qwen on-prem.

At Skywalker Sound I am the ML platform engineer for four research scientists and one MLE. Shared Ray pools cover on-prem A100s, CoreWeave, GCP, AWS, and Mac Studios. A VAE that took five months on GCP came down to weeks on-prem, with faster turnaround on the model architecture. The measured version is in the [Skywalker impact notes](skywalker-sound/key-projects.md#impact).

At Procore, four teams adopted one model lifecycle, and model deployment went from four weeks to one week. Data scientists built the ACV models. I optimized, containerized, and deployed them. How the [$8M+ upsell figure](procore/modeling-work.md#acv-prediction) is counted stays with that work.

Before Procore I led eight engineers at Disney/Hulu, after data engineering at Glassdoor and Autodesk.
