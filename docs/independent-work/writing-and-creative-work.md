# Writing and Creative Work

## *FRACTURED SKY*

*FRACTURED SKY: THE DISCOVERY* is an adult science-fiction novel and the opening of a planned trilogy. I wrote the first draft in six weeks. Then came the much longer part: revision, feedback, more revision, and learning how publishing actually works. I am now preparing targeted literary-agent queries and sending them manually.

Writing a novel uses a surprising number of the same muscles as engineering. You hold a large system in your head, find contradictions, take feedback, and keep rewriting without losing the thing that made you care about it in the first place. The outcome is uncertain and you still have to keep going.

## Music and Storytelling Experiments

I also write autobiographical songs and experiment with AI-assisted production. The tools can help with arrangement ideas, vocal direction, and production concepts. The story and the emotional decisions still have to come from me.

## Product Storytelling

Building my own products forced me out of the backend-engineer comfort zone. I had to learn positioning, App Store copy, press outreach, support, accessibility language, and how to explain a release without overselling it. With NorthPaw, I am also careful about one specific line: AI may help with production work, but the safety guidance itself comes from deterministic logic, not generated advice.
